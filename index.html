How it works

Live in a day, not a quarter.

No integration project, no engineering time, nothing to maintain on your side.

01

Tell us what you cover

Leagues, teams, competitions — whatever your audience follows. We set up the fixtures and keep the data current.

02

Paste one snippet

An iframe on any page: a widget, a sidebar, a dedicated section. Set your colours and logo and it inherits your site's look, light or dark.

03

Readers start playing

They predict, check the leaderboard, and come back for the next round. You get the repeat visits and the registered users.

Game modes

Four formats. Or one built for you.

Run a single mode or several at once. If none of them fit what you have in mind, we'll build a custom one.

Score Predictor

Predict the exact scoreline

The classic format. Points for the right result, more for the exact score. Simple to explain and instantly familiar to any sports audience.

Betting Markets

Several calls per match

Match result, over/under, both teams to score — you choose which markets run and what each is worth. Markets adapt to the sport: winning-margin ranges for basketball and baseball, point ranges for handball.

Ranking

Order the teams

Readers rank teams by a metric you choose — goals, expected goals, points. A different kind of thinking from picking winners, and it rewards genuine knowledge.

Lineup Predictor

Pick the starting eleven

Readers build an XI on a visual pitch in any formation, then answer bonus questions on the first goalscorer, man of the match and first substitution. Pulls the next fixture automatically.

Data widgets

Stats content, no sign-up required.

Not every reader wants to play. These are pure content — drop them into an article or sidebar and they work immediately.

Squad Analytics

How settled is each squad?

A league-wide table ranking every club on distinct players started, share of minutes covered, number of different lineups fielded, average changes per game, plus nationalities and squad age. Tap a club for the player-by-player breakdown.

Top Scorers

A scorer chart with context

Filters an ordinary table can't manage: goal type — whether it put the team ahead, levelled the game, pulled one back or extended a lead — and the minute it was scored, from kick-off through stoppage time.

Why publishers use it

Retention you can see, inventory you can sell.

Habit, not a one-off visit

Match previews get read once. A prediction round gives readers a reason to come back before kick-off, check the result afterwards, and return for the next fixture.

The users are yours

Everyone who registers stays in your workspace — available for your newsletter and remarketing. We can also connect to your existing login so nobody signs up twice.

Sponsorable by design

Brand the competition, the leaderboard, or place banners inside the widget. A returning weekly audience is an easier sponsorship sell than a page view.

Genuinely free to play

No wagering, no deposits, no money staked by readers. It's a prediction contest, not betting — which keeps it appropriate for a general sports audience.

Any sport, any competition

Football, basketball, baseball, handball, ice hockey, esports. Markets adjust to the sport rather than forcing a football-shaped game onto everything.

Nothing to maintain

We handle fixtures, results and scoring. Your editorial workflow doesn't change, and there's no dashboard anyone on your team is obliged to log into.
